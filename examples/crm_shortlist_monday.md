# CRM Evaluation Shortlist
Date: Monday, November 9, 2026
Team: 20-person outbound and inbound sales team

## Shortlisted Candidates
- Attio: $34/user/month. Modern data-driven CRM with custom objects and automated enrichment.
- HubSpot: $20/seat/month. Starter Sales Hub, solid inbound sequences and marketing integration.
- Close: $49/user/month. Built specifically for high-velocity outbound calling and SMS sequences.
- Pipedrive: $14/user/month. Clean visual pipeline view, good for straightforward deal tracking.
- Folk: $18/user/month. Lightweight contact manager with LinkedIn integration.

## Key Decisions
Shortlisted these 5 tools to evaluate pricing tiers, API limits, and team workflows before making final procurement decision next week.
